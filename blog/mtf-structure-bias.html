MTF Structure Bias

Educational article. Not financial advice. Trading involves substantial risk of loss.

This piece covers the core ideas behind a mechanical approach to the topic. CHARTIA members get the full curriculum, Day + Swing trade plans, private community with same-day coach replies, and ongoing structure analysis.

Key principle: define structure first, mark high-quality supply and demand, wait for liquidity where it matters, and only take trades that meet a strict minimum R:R with clear invalidation.

Multi-timeframe bias means the higher timeframe sets direction (BOS / CHoCH), while the lower timeframe provides the entry at a refined zone. Fighting HTF structure is the fastest way to bleed a prop account.
