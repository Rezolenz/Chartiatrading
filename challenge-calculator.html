The hidden cost of playing it too safe

Same 40% win rate, 2:1 R:R strategy — drastically different timelines.

Conservative · 0.5% risk

  • Pass rate ~98% (almost guaranteed)
  • Expected cost: ~1 attempt
  • ~150 trades to target
  • Time: 7–12 months at 10 trades/month
  • Hidden cost: months of $0 income + psychological drag

Strategic · 1.5–3% risk

  • Still high pass probability with good process
  • 1–3 attempts expected
  • ~40–60 trades to target
  • Time: 6–10 weeks possible
  • Start earning real payouts sooner
