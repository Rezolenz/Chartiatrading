The Method

Systematic Supply & Demand

A complete framework built around institutional footprints — not retail Myths.

01

Market Structure

Map higher highs, higher lows, breaks of structure, and change of character. Know the directional bias before hunting zones.

02

Supply & Demand

Locate fresh institutional zones — the last opposing impulse before displacement. Refine entries where price is most likely to react.

03

Liquidity

Identify equal highs/lows, stop clusters, and inducement. Institutions hunt liquidity before the real move — we wait for the sweep.

04

Risk & Execution

Define invalidation at the structural level. Size for survival. Target measured moves and opposing liquidity. Minimum 1:5 R:R.
