Curriculum

Trading Learning
Roadmap

From foundational market mechanics to institutional supply & demand, Day + Swing trade plans, testing, and scaling. Advance through structured modules at your own pace.

01

Foundations

Market mechanics, sessions, and how price is auctioned. Build the base before structure.

What you learn

  • Sessions & liquidity hours (Asia, London, NY)
  • Bid/ask, spreads, and order types
  • Candlestick context (not pattern hunting)
  • How institutions participate vs retail

Outcomes

  • Read a chart without indicators
  • Know when liquidity is highest
  • Understand why setups fail outside session context
02

Market Structure

Trend, range, break of structure, and change of character. Know who is in control.

What you learn

  • Swing highs/lows mapping
  • Break of structure (BOS)
  • Change of character (CHoCH)
  • Multi-timeframe alignment

Outcomes

  • Define directional bias objectively
  • Avoid counter-structure entries
  • Frame every trade inside structure
03

Supply & Demand

Institutional zones, imbalances, and refinement — where price is programmed to react.

What you learn

  • Fresh vs consumed zones
  • Imbalance / inefficiency
  • Zone quality checklist
  • Entry refinement techniques

Outcomes

  • Mark only high-probability zones
  • Ignore weak or mid-range levels
  • Enter with defined invalidation
04

Liquidity & Risk Execution

Stop hunts, inducement, position sizing, and mechanical R:R rules.

What you learn

  • Liquidity pools & sweeps
  • Inducement recognition
  • Position sizing algorithms
  • 1:5+ risk-to-reward framework

Outcomes

  • Wait for liquidity before entry
  • Size every trade for survival
  • Execute only when R:R qualifies
05

Day + Swing Trade Plans

Ready-to-use mechanical plans for intraday and multi-day holds.

What you get

  • Day trading session checklist
  • Swing trading plan
  • When to sit out / invalidation rules
  • Target and management logic

Outcomes

  • Same process every session
  • Fits lifestyle or full-time trading
06

Testing & Journaling (CHARTIA)

Prove the edge with data. Journal templates and review system so progress is mechanical.

What you learn

  • Backtest process
  • Forward testing rules
  • Structured trade journal
  • Weekly / monthly review protocol

Outcomes

  • Trust your numbers, not feelings
  • Know what to improve next
07

Prop Challenges & Scaling

Apply the same process inside funding challenges and grow capital without changing rules.

What you learn

  • Challenge risk math
  • Drawdown survival
  • Scaling plan across accounts
  • Psychology under evaluation pressure

Outcomes

  • Pass and stay funded
  • Compound process first, then size
